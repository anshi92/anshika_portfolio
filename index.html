My approach to AI Business Translation

I don't start with “Where can we use AI?” I start with “What problem are we trying to solve, and is AI the right answer?”

01 — UnderstandUnderstand the customer, process, pain point and business context.
02 — ChallengeAssess AI against simpler options such as elimination, digitisation, automation or process change.
03 — ShapeTranslate the problem into a clear use case, requirements, success criteria and data needs.
04 — BuildWork across business, product, data and technology teams to shape a practical solution.
05 — AdoptInvolve users, test workflows and define where human judgement remains essential.
06 — MeasureTrack quality, time, throughput, customer experience, employee impact and business value.

How I would approach an existing business problem

From operational challenge to AI opportunity

When working with a mortgage business or another regulated organisation, I would start with an existing operational problem rather than assuming that AI is the answer.


  • 1. Understand the problem
    Speak with customers, frontline employees, process owners and product teams to understand where the current process creates friction, delay, rework or inconsistent outcomes.
  • 2. Map the current process
    Document the end-to-end journey, including systems, data, hand-offs, decision points, exceptions and areas requiring human judgement.
  • 3. Identify the root cause
    Separate the actual business problem from the symptoms. The solution may turn out to be process simplification, better data, automation, policy change or digitisation rather than AI.
  • 4. Assess the AI opportunity
    Where AI could add value, define the use case, required data, expected business value, feasibility and the tasks that should remain with humans.
  • 5. Design and test the workflow
    Work with business, product, data and technology teams to design the AI-enabled process, test it with users and define clear acceptance criteria, controls and escalation paths.
  • 6. Measure and improve
    Track outcomes such as handling time, quality, rework, throughput, customer experience and employee experience, and use those results to decide whether the solution should be improved or scaled.

My principle: start with the problem, understand the process, challenge the need for AI, and only then design the technology-enabled solution.
