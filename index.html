My approach to AI Business Translation

I don't start with "Where can we use AI?" I start with "What problem are we trying to solve, and is AI the right answer?"

01 — Understand Understand the customer, process, pain point and business context.
02 — Challenge Ask whether AI is actually the right solution versus simplification, digitisation, automation or process change.
03 — Shape Translate the problem into a clear AI use case, requirements, success criteria and data needs.
04 — Build Work across business, product, data and technology teams to design a practical solution.
05 — Adopt Involve users, test workflows and define where human judgement remains essential.
06 — Measure Track business value through quality, time, throughput, customer experience and employee impact.

Illustrative AI use case — Mortgage Operations

AI-assisted mortgage document intake

Illustrative concept — not an NN project.


A possible AI-enabled workflow could help mortgage operations teams process incoming documentation:


  • Classify incoming documents
  • Extract relevant information
  • Check whether required information is complete
  • Identify inconsistencies or exceptions
  • Route cases requiring human review
  • Keep final judgement with appropriately authorised employees

Potential measures could include handling time, first-time-right rate, manual effort, exception rate, turnaround time and quality.
